# Ensign text

All 20 ensigns in the order the selection page shows them. **Card** is the text on
the card now, from `Ensigns.cs`. **Effect** is what the code does.

## White Ensign (8 pts)

**Card:** Guns, missiles, torpedoes and mortars reach 3× further

**Effect:** ×3 range on all four, SAMs included. CIWS is not affected.

## Stars and Stripes (5 pts)

**Card:** Guns do 4x more damage, but rate of fire is cut by 50%

**Effect:** ×4 damage, ×0.5 rate of fire, so ×2 damage per second. Shell speed is unchanged.

## Icelandic Coast Guard Ensign (5 pts)

**Card:** Guns do up to 6× damage to ships as they get closer

**Effect:** ×6 at zero range, falling evenly to ×1 at the gun's maximum range. Ships only.

## Soviet Naval Ensign (7 pts)

**Card:** All SAMs, ASMs and AAMs do 2× damage

**Effect:** ×2 on every fleet missile, SAMs included.

## Indian Naval Ensign (5 pts)

**Card:** Your ASMs move twice as fast. 

**Effect:** Anti-ship missiles double their speed. 

## Dutch Tricolour (8 pts)

**Card:** CIWS range ×100. Guns aim 4× as well, and fire and reload 2× as fast. SAMs and air-to-air missiles are disabled

**Effect:** As written. Aim means a quarter of the usual error. Launchers that also hold other rounds keep firing those.

## St Andrew's Flag (5 pts)

**Card:** Mortar rounds are EMPs instead of warheads: radars and fire control go dark. Each mortar rank widens the pulse and makes it last longer. Sovremenny, Slava, Kirov and Kiev only

**Effect:** Enemy ships in the radius lose their radars and hold fire. 2 nm and 60 s at rank 0, up to 6 nm and 3 min at rank 20.

## Italian Naval Ensign (5 pts)

**Card:** Torpedoes run and turn 3× as fast, reach 3× as far, hit 1.5× as hard, and can target ships

**Effect:** As written. The turn rate rises with speed so the turning circle stays the same size.

## Swedish Naval Ensign (3 pts)

**Card:** All torpedoes are wire-guided for half their range, and can target ships

**Effect:** The wire is cut at half range, and torpedoes run at wire speed until then. Ships get 8 wires, and salvos stay at 3.

## German Naval Ensign (6 pts)

**Card:** All torpedo tubes are replaced by the EA-6B's ECM jammer. Any Torpedo bonuses are replaced by Jammer bonuses. 

**Effect:** Tubes are replaced by the ALQ-99 jammer, and the Torpedoes line by a 20-rank Jammers line.

## Australian White Ensign (4 pts)

**Card:** Double the number of aircraft you get per card or escort. 

**Effect:** Every flight, from the start or a chest, has twice the aircraft.

## Estonian Naval Ensign (3 pts)

**Card:** Your aircraft's radar signatures are reduced by 95%

**Effect:** RCS ×0.05.

## Argentine Ensign (6 pts)

**Card:** Unguided bombs do 10× damage and are 10x more accurate. Strike aircraft carry only those, with a quarter of the radar signature. Start with a free strike pair

**Effect:** As written, with the free pair only if no strike flight is sailing. A Kiev's deck aircraft can still carry guided weapons.

## Greek Ensign (7 pts)

**Card:** You can only select one type of escort, but you get 5 of them. 

**Effect:** As written.

## New Zealand White Ensign (6 pts)

**Card:** Ship and Aircraft escort cards awarded during a run provide you with 2 of the selected escort.

**Effect:** As written.

## Turkish Naval Ensign (7 pts)

**Card:** The flagship ignores one missile, bomb or torpedo, and one more every 5 levels

**Effect:** Starts with 1 charge, +1 at each level divisible by 5. Near misses that would harm the flagship spend a charge, and shells do not.

## Dannebrog (4 pts)

**Card:** Each time an enemy attack gets within 3nm of your fleet, your damage is increased by 20% for the rest of the run. This effect can only occur once per 10s. 

**Effect:** A close call is any enemy weapon, gun rounds included, within 3 nm of any fleet ship, counted at most once per 10 s. The bonus adds up, so 5 calls is +100%. All your weapons get it.

## Norwegian Ensign (10 pts)

**Card:** Cards are at least Rare

**Effect:** Any lower roll is raised to Rare.

## Irish Tricolour (5 pts)

**Card:** A fourth card at every level-up, 3 more rerolls and 3 more banishes

**Effect:** As written.

## Spanish Ensign (4 pts)

**Card:** Start with four free cards

**Effect:** The run starts with four level-ups waiting.

# Pairs that cannot fly together

- German with Italian or Swedish
- Greek with Australian, Argentine or New Zealand
- Dutch with Australian

# Wording notes

- **Australian:** "a free fighter pair" undersells it, because with no flights you get that fighter flight twice. If a flight is already a pair, that is four aircraft.
- **Italian and Swedish:** "can target ships" only matters until the first Torpedoes card, since any rank of that line already allows it.
- **Dannebrog:** the card never says what a close call is.
- **Stars and Stripes:** "300% more damage" is easy to misread as ×3, when it is ×4.
- **St Andrew's and Argentine:** these are full paragraphs, far longer than the rest.
- **German:** "carry the EA-6B's jammer instead, which you aim" reads awkwardly and assumes you know the aircraft.
